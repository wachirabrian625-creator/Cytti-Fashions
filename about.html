Our story

Pre-loved, perfectly chosen.

Cytti Fashions Store was born from a simple belief — great style shouldn't cost the earth or your savings. From our shop in Nairobi, founder Fibbie Wanjiku hand-picks every piece so you can shop with confidence.

Whether you're after a denim jacket with character, a printed blouse, or weekend sneakers, our collections are refreshed often and curated with care.

Meet the owner

Fibbie Wanjiku

Fibbie is the heart of Cytti Fashions Store. With an eye for quality and a love for storytelling through clothing, she personally selects every sneaker, shirt and pair of jeans on the floor. Customers come for the pieces — they stay for her warmth.

Drop by the shop in Nairobi or reach out directly — Fibbie usually replies within minutes on WhatsApp.
